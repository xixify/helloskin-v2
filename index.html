    Assumptions in this preview

    1. Ohsogo was the layout reference only. This page takes its retail structure: search-first header, mega-menu, category bubbles, hero slider, trust strip, product rails, brand tiles, combos, concern tabs, ingredient tiles, a shoppable routine, reviews, popular searches and SEO text. The name, logo, colours and copy are all Hello Skin's own.
    2. All prices, discounts and combo prices are placeholders in BDT. Your sheet has no prices. Struck-through "regular" prices and brand "up to X% off" offers are examples to show the layout.
    3. Concerns and ingredients are matched from each product's name and one-line description, so a few may be missing or misplaced until full ingredient lists are added.
    4. Customer reviews are sample layouts, clearly labelled. Replace them with real reviews (for example from your Facebook page) before launch. Star ratings and review counts are placeholders.
    5. New arrivals, bestsellers and combos are hand-picked to show the layout. Tell me the real ones.
    6. Photos are your real files after the Phase 1 fixes. Strawberry Dew Spotlight Strobe Cream has no photo and shows a styled placeholder.
    7. Built only with Elementor, Elementor Pro and free WooCommerce. To keep to that, this version drops the wishlist, order tracking, store locator, "Buy now" button, percentage-off and New/Bestseller badges on cards, hover image swap, the free-delivery progress bar, cart-drawer quantity buttons, one-click "add the whole set", and "More from this brand" on product pages. Those need extra plugins or custom code.
    8. How it maps to Elementor: the header search is the Pro Search widget with live results, the category menu is the Pro Menu widget with dropdown content, rails are Loop Carousels, tabs are Nested Tabs, the banner is the Slides widget, the routine photo uses the Hotspot widget, and the cart drawer is the Menu Cart. Shop filters are WooCommerce's free Brand, Price and Active Filters, and sorting uses WooCommerce's own options. Brands use WooCommerce's built-in Brands; concerns and ingredients are product tags.
    9. Combo kits are their own products with a regular and a sale price, so they work with free WooCommerce. They don't track the stock of the items inside.
    10. Checkout, account and policy pages are not connected in this preview. Cart, search, filters, sorting, tabs and the slider all work.
